# Learned detection baseline vs the classical detector

Generated by `pairbench.experiments.learned_baseline`, 108 scenes / 216 scene-sides / 935 GT faces. 2 seed(s) x 2000 training steps per fold, CPU only (8 threads).

## Summary

**Scene-disjoint:** held-out F1 0.902 (mean over seeds) vs classical 0.970. Paired bundle bootstrap: delta -0.087, 95% CI [-0.143, -0.045], p = 0.000 (separated).

**Family-disjoint (held-out family):** held-out F1 0.425 (mean over seeds) vs classical 0.970. Paired bundle bootstrap: delta -0.615, 95% CI [-0.673, -0.556], p = 0.000 (separated).

F1 here is the mean over seeds; the Headline table reports the seed-0 pooled F1.

In-sample F1 0.931, scene-disjoint 0.902, family-disjoint 0.425 (difference +0.477). The classical arm needs only the family's DXF template.

Position-anchored recall: classical 1.000, scene-disjoint 0.853, family-disjoint 0.122.

Held-out F1 moves +0.275 between 750 and 6000 training steps (see 'Was it trained long enough?').

Training folds (seed 0): 72-74 scenes / 505-614 faces in the scene split; 40-92 scenes / 403-770 faces in the family split; 5 profile families, the smallest 4 scenes; 2 seed(s).


## What is being compared

Both arms are scored on `gt.csv`'s per-side counts (TP per side = min(pred, gt), as in `experiments.detect`) and on the position-anchored re-link against `gt_correspondence.csv`. Classical numbers are read from `b1_detections_pool.json`.

Training labels are the 847 anchors in `gt_correspondence.csv` (90.6% of the 935 GT faces). The other 88 (9.4%) were never produced by the classical detector, so they are unlabelled (background) in training; scoring uses the complete `gt.csv` counts.

## Position-anchored recall

Fraction of `gt_correspondence.csv` anchors that re-link to a detection under `gt.resolve_anchor_indices` with the default 25 mm gate, as in the correspondence scorer.

| arm | linked | anchors | recall |
|---|---|---|---|
| classical `pool` | 870 | 870 | **1.000** |
| learned (scene-disjoint) | 742 | 870 | **0.853** |
| learned (family-disjoint) | 106 | 870 | **0.122** |

Anchor coordinates are centroids of classical detections, so this metric favours the classical arm; the count metric has no such bias.

## Was it trained long enough?

One scene-disjoint fold, re-trained from scratch at several step budgets (`learned_convergence.csv`). The main run uses 2000 steps per fold.

| steps | inner-val threshold | held-out P | R | F1 | train time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 750 | 0.400 | 0.883 | 0.555 | 0.682 | 5.5 min |
| 1500 | 0.475 | 0.964 | 0.652 | 0.778 | 10.5 min |
| 3000 | 0.500 | 1.000 | 0.726 | 0.841 | 21.9 min |
| 6000 | 0.275 | 0.963 | 0.951 | 0.957 | 41.6 min |

From 750 to 6000 steps the held-out F1 moves 0.682 -> 0.957 at 7.6x the training cost.
